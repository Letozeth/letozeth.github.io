## The world

- Tenebreth was once called Lucereth. Then its greatest heroes opened something in the far west that should have stayed closed. Demons and spirits poured out, a black cloud covered the sky, and half the lands and their people were lost before anyone could organise a defence.
What remains of humanity has fallen back to the east, to **Epistrepho**: the last city, built around a Grand Cathedral on a cliff. Its name means *to turn back*. Paladins and Priests hold its walls, Mages lend their combined power, and adventurers keep the surrounding land clear.

## You
- You are a devout villager who lost everything to a demon attack: your family, your home, and your memory. You wake in the backstreets of Epistrepho with nothing but rags and a wedding ring, choose a new name, and decide what to live for.

## Four ways to fight the dark

- Every faction fights the Darkness in its own way, and no method is off-limits if it is used against Evil. At the end of the prologue you seek out one of them and receive its **Blessing**.

- The Church:
Priests who heal anyone in need, and Paladins who hold the wall.

- The Mages Consortium:
The four elemental schools of Fire, Water, Air and Earth, united under one banner.

- The Guild:
Warriors, Rogues and Rangers. The old adventurers' organisation, now home to fighters with nowhere else to go.

- The Occult Brotherhood:
Mages who turn the Darkness against itself, always wrestling demons for control of their magic.

## Blessings
- A Blessing is almost like a class that helps define your build, it doesn't lock you in and it doesn't limit you. It gives you starting attributes and a talent point head start in the given talent tree. Seven talent trees share one pool of points, so a Warrior can still go all-in on Fire.
## Warrior Blessing Example
- Strength +10
- Endurance +10
- Spirit +5
- Physical Talent Tree +6 (counted as spent points)
- 5 Max Health per level
- 2 Max Mana per level.

## What it plays like

- **Real-time combat** from a top-down camera, with skills on hotkeys and a dodge jump that makes you untouchable (i-frames) while you're in the air, also serves as a jump.
- **Co-op** with friends through Steam lobbies.
- **Build freedom**, across seven talent trees: Physical, Fire, Water, Air, Earth, Holy and Dark.
- **Loot worth chasing**, with five rarity tiers from Common to Legendary, rolled affixes, and crafting that lets you steer rolls instead of only gambling on drops. As well as planned Unique loot with preset affixes and build defining custom affixes.
- **Alchemy, Metalworking and Enchanting**, with herbs and metals gathered out in the world.
- **Dungeons** generated floor by floor, with difficulty tiers for better rewards.
- **A Bestiary** that fills in as you hunt, for the completionists.
- **A long level curve to 100.** Don't expect to hit 100 unless you grind.
