## New Weapons Models
- A set of 9 weapons with 3 sets of skins.
- Tome, Dagger, Axe, Sword, Staff, Greatsword, Crossbow, Scepter & Shield.
- T4 weapons will receive new models, unfortunately as I'm working alone on the models and animations, I have to resort to a lot of reskins.
- None of these models are to be considered final.
- The Tome as well as the crystal in the staff and the gem in the Scepter will all have assigned shader materials in-game for further effects.
