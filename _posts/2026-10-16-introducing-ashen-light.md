Ashen Light is set in Tenebreth, a world that used to be called Lucereth before the Darkness took it. A dark miasma hides the sun, demons rule most of the land, and what's left of humanity holds out in the east, in a city built around a cathedral on a cliff.

You play as a villager who has lost everything to a demon attack, including their memory. You wake up in that last city, pick a new name, join one of four factions, and head out to push the dark back.

## What's built so far

The game already has a lot of its foundation in place:

- The Act I Crossroads town area, with vendors, crafting stations and storage
- Real-time combat with skills on hotkeys and a dodge jump
- Seven talent trees in the works, with no class locking you out of any of them
- Loot with rarity tiers and rolled affixes, plus crafting
- The first dungeon, the Catacombs, which is three floors of the undead
- A day and night cycle with moon phases, wind and rain, and campfires to rest at

There's plenty still ahead, from the story acts beyond the first region to the enemies that will fill them.
